FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the production capacity of DPP-320 machine?

The DPP-320 produces 2,400 to 9,600 blisters per hour with a forming area of 310×160mm and stroke range of 40-180mm. It supports PVC, PVDC, and cold-form aluminum.

What packaging materials are compatible with your blister machines?

Our machines work with PVC rigid film, PVC/PVDC composite film, PTP aluminum foil, and cold-forming aluminum foil (OPA/AL/PVC). We also supply all these materials for your convenience.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship globally with FOB, CIF, and DDP terms available. We have exported to 20+ countries and handle all export documentation for a smooth customs clearance.

What certifications do your machines have?

Our machines are CE and GMP certified. We also provide IQ/OQ/PQ validation documentation support for pharmaceutical clients to meet regulatory requirements.

Can I get samples of packaging materials before ordering?

Yes, we offer sample films for testing. Contact us through our quote form with your requirements and we will send appropriate samples for your evaluation.

What is the production capacity of the NJP-1200D?

The NJP-1200D produces up to 72,000 capsules per hour with filling accuracy of ±3% for Western powders and ±4% for TCM/herbal extracts. It handles capsule sizes 00# to 5#.

What is the difference between NJP-1200D and CGNT-209?

NJP-1200D is fully automatic (72,000 caps/hr, 5.5 kW, 380/220V) with Siemens PLC, sealed rotary table, and Becker vacuum pump. CGNT-209 is a semi-automatic double-head machine (40,000 caps/hr, 2.2 kW, 380V) with Siemens PLC swivel touchscreen, double-row capsule trays, and parallel three-station design — ideal for medium-batch production.
